About

PhD candidate in Nutrition and Metabolism (minor in Quantitative Biology) at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, concurrently completing an M.S. in Computer Science (Machine Learning track) at Georgia Tech. I build machine learning and computational pipelines for high-dimensional biomedical data, including single-cell transcriptomics (140K+ cells), microbiome and LC–MS/MS metabolomics, and biomedical text and knowledge graphs, and connect them to experimental work on diet–microbe–host metabolism and metabolic liver disease. Co-author on publications in PNAS and Nature Metabolism.
